Easy Notes

What is a Unit?

A unit is a fixed standard used to measure a physical quantity. Example: metre (m) for length and kilogram (kg) for mass.

SI Base Units

Length – metre (m)
Mass – kilogram (kg)
Time – second (s)
Electric Current – ampere (A)
Temperature – kelvin (K)
Amount of Substance – mole (mol)
Luminous Intensity – candela (cd)

Derived Units

Some quantities are obtained from base units. Examples: Force → Newton (N) Energy → Joule (J) Power → Watt (W) Pressure → Pascal (Pa)

Important Concepts

📏 Significant Figures

Significant figures are the meaningful digits in a measured value. They indicate the precision of a measurement.

🎯 Accuracy & Precision

Accuracy means how close a measurement is to the true value. Precision means how close repeated measurements are to each other.

📐 Dimensional Formula

Force = [M L T⁻²]
Energy = [M L² T⁻²]
Power = [M L² T⁻³]

MCQs

1. SI unit of Force is:

A) Joule
B) Newton
C) Watt
D) Pascal

Answer: B) Newton

2. SI unit of Time is:

A) Minute
B) Hour
C) Second
D) Day

Answer: C) Second
