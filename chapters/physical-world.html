Easy Notes

What is Physics?

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, force, space and time. It helps us understand how the universe works from atoms to galaxies.

Importance of Physics

Physics is the foundation of engineering, medicine, communication, transportation, computers, satellites and modern technology.

Branches of Physics

Thermodynamics
Optics
Electromagnetism
Modern Physics
Nuclear Physics
Astrophysics

Important Points

⭐ Physics

Physics explains natural phenomena using observations and experiments.

⭐ Scientific Method

Every scientific theory must be tested experimentally.

⭐ Daily Life

Physics is used in electricity, communication, transportation, medicine and space science.
