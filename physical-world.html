What is Physics?

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, force, motion, space and time. It explains how everything in the universe works—from tiny atoms to huge galaxies.

🎯 Importance of Physics

  • Explains natural phenomena.
  • Used in Engineering.
  • Used in Medical Science.
  • Very important for JEE & NEET.

📚 Branches of Physics

  • Mechanics
  • Thermodynamics
  • Optics
  • Electricity
  • Magnetism
  • Modern Physics

📝 Important Formula

Density = Mass / Volume

💡 Did You Know?

Albert Einstein developed the Theory of Relativity.
