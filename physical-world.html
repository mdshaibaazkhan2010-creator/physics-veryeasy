What is Physics?

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, force, space, and time. It helps us understand how the universe works.

Importance of Physics

  • Explains natural phenomena.
  • Helps develop modern technology.
  • Forms the foundation of engineering and medicine.
  • Very important for CBSE, JEE, and NEET.
