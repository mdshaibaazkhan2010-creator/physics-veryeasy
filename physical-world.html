Easy Notes

What is Physics?

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, force, space and time. It helps us understand how the universe works, from tiny atoms to massive galaxies.

Importance of Physics

Physics is the foundation of engineering, medicine, communication, transportation, space research and modern technology.

Branches of Physics

Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Optics, Modern Physics, Nuclear Physics and Astrophysics.

Important Points

⭐ Point 1

Physics explains the laws of nature using observations and experiments.

⭐ Point 2

Every scientific theory must be tested through experiments.

⭐ Point 3

Physics plays an important role in engineering, medicine, computers and space science.

MCQs

1. Physics is the study of:

A) Plants
B) Matter and Energy
C) Animals
D) History

Answer: B) Matter and Energy

2. Which branch studies motion?

A) Mechanics
B) Biology
C) Chemistry
D) Botany

Answer: A) Mechanics
